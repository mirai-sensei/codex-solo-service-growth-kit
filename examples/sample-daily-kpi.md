Date: 2026-06-01
Owner: Solo operator

This fictional example shows how to fill in the daily KPI template without storing customer names, email addresses, payment records, or private consultation notes.

## Conversion

| Step | Count | Rate | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| Free form started | 31 | 48% | The form headline seems clear enough. |
| Free form completed | 18 | 28% | Drop-off may be caused by too many fields. |
| Paid offer clicked | 7 | 11% | Offer CTA appears after the free result. |
| Paid purchase or booking | 2 | 3% | Two paid actions from the free path. |

## One Finding

The largest visible bottleneck is between free form started and free form completed.

## One Next Action

Shorten the free form or move optional questions after the first response so more visitors can complete the free entry point.
