# Sample Funnel Map

Project: Fictional coaching service
Main offer: 45-minute paid consultation
Free entry point: Free self-check worksheet
Primary paid CTA: Book a paid consultation

## Audience

People who feel stuck and want a practical next step.

People looking for emergency, legal, medical, or financial advice are not targeted.

## Positioning

Remembered as the clear next-step consultation for people who are overwhelmed but ready to act.

## Flow

| Step | Page or asset | Visitor intent | Desired action | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Short video | Find relief | Click profile link | Hook too vague |
| 2 | Free worksheet | Understand situation | Complete worksheet | Too many fields |
| 3 | Result page | Get direction | Click paid CTA | CTA too cold |
| 4 | Booking page | Decide | Book consultation | Offer unclear |

## Bottlenecks

| Bottleneck | Evidence | Low-risk test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free worksheet completion | Many starts, few completions | Reduce fields from 8 to 4 |

## Do Not Touch

- Production secrets.
- Payment settings.
- Customer data.
- Product prices.
- External service credentials.

## Next Safe Improvement

Rewrite the free worksheet intro so the visitor understands it takes under 2 minutes and leads to one clear next step.
